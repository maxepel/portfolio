# Film archive asset credits

## Projector and film reels

- **Vintage film projector**, by **Lowpolylines Parussa**.
- Source: https://www.blendkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/1fbb9c68-51aa-45c8-9dd9-678373cc2bcb/
- Source asset version: `1d35f2d7-21ea-4069-9f13-22f9a1b090fd`.
- License: [Blendkit Royalty Free](https://www.blendkit.com/docs/licenses/).
- The source is free to download but is not CC0 or open-source geometry.
- Derived files: `vintage-projector.glb` and `archive-reel.glb`.
- Modifications: mesh simplification, flange normal cleanup, smaller textures,
  coordinate normalization, separation of static projector and rotating reels,
  adaptation to the site's mounting animation, restoration of the enamel tint
  lost in the source's glTF export, and adjusted steel material response.
- The models are incorporated into this portfolio experience; they are not
  offered as an independent asset pack.
